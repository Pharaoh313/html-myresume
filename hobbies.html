My Hobbies & Interests

Watching anime has been a pastime since I was a child. My current favorite is "That Time I Got Reincarnated As A Slime" but my all-time favorite is the "DragonBall" series. I enjoy exploring different genres from action-packed shonen to thoughtful slice-of-life stories.

🏀Sports Fan

I enjoy watching and playing football and basketball, with a growing interest in baseball. My favorite player is LeBron James - his leadership on and off the court is truly inspiring. Sports teach valuable lessons about teamwork, perseverance, and strategic thinking.

🥗Food Explorer

I am a vegetarian, so I love exploring vegan restaurants and those offering creative vegetarian options. Discovering new plant-based cuisines and flavors is always an adventure. I enjoy trying everything from local farm-to-table restaurants to international vegetarian dishes.
